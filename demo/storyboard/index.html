Your voice

Narration comes from your Chatterbox clone on Comfy Cloud (the miles-voiceover skill): each line is generated three times against the mood reference shown on its beat, checked with Whisper for wrong words and breathy tails, then the best take is trimmed and levelled. GPU time only, no partner credits, nothing stored with a voice vendor. I will play you line 1 before generating the rest.

What I need from you

  1. The seven lines and their moods. Edit any wording you like; the beats stretch to fit. 100 spoken words now.
  2. Length. With the experiment beat added the cut runs about 55–57 s against the 50 s target. To get back to 50, the cleanest cut is merging beats 1 and 2 into one split frame (shapes on the left, cards on the right) under one line; say if you want that, or if 55 s is fine.
  3. Lane on screen. Her first name appears in line 2, on the sign in beats 4 and 6, and as "for Lane" on the outro. Say if any of those should go.
